Hi Everyone,

We are delighted to launch the Midas Employee Exchange Program, a structured opportunity for colleagues in Pakistan and Sri Lanka to work for a period in each other's country, share knowledge and strengthen our regional teams.

               
HOST
Pakistan
Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad
               
HOST
Sri Lanka
Colombo and regional offices
What you gain:
  • Duration: 3 to 6 months, agreed with the host department.
  • Fully covered: flights, visa, accommodation and relocation allowance.
  • Salary protected: your role and pay continue in your home country.
  • Real exposure: hands-on work in our busiest markets and sites.
  • Certificate and a strong reference on your return.

Who can apply

  • Minimum 2 years of service with Midas Safety.
  • Confirmed employee with a satisfactory performance record.
  • Good command of English, plus Urdu or Sinhala for day-to-day work.
  • Available for the full exchange period and willing to travel.
